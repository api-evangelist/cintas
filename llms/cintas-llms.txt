# Cintas Corporation

> Cintas Corporation (NASDAQ: CTAS) is a Fortune 500 provider of uniform rental and facility
> services, first aid and safety products and training, and fire protection services across
> North America. Cintas publishes no public developer portal, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL
> contract, and no /.well-known/ discovery documents. Machine integration with Cintas is
> account-managed: X12 EDI trading-partner onboarding arranged through a Cintas sales/account
> team, plus the myCintas customer sign-in for billing and service management. This file is
> GENERATED by API Evangelist from the catalog record — it is not published by Cintas.
