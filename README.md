# Cintas (cintas)

Cintas Corporation is a Fortune 500 provider of uniform rental, facility services, first aid and safety products, and fire protection services. Cintas does not currently publish a public developer portal; B2B integrations (ordering, EDI, route management, billing) are delivered to enterprise customers through the myCintas portal, the Cintas Partner Connect program, and account-managed EDI relationships.

## APIs

No public APIs have been documented yet. Cintas does not operate an open developer program. Common ways customers integrate with Cintas systems today include:

- The myCintas customer portal for ordering and account management
- EDI relationships negotiated through enterprise account managers
- Procurement integrations through punchout catalogs (Coupa, Ariba, etc.)

If you have access to a Cintas integration spec or a published API, contributions are welcome.
